Data and confidentiality

Your data stays yours.

What we sign, what we can open, and what happens to your files when the work ends.

  1. Before we start

    We sign first

    An NDA, and a written list of what we can open and which services your data may pass through.

  2. While we work

    Inside your systems

    We work in your own accounts where we can, with the least access the task needs.

  3. When we finish

    We delete our copies

    Within 30 days, confirmed in writing. Sooner if you ask.

Eight commitments, in writing

You would be trusting us with deal files and investor numbers. This is what we commit to before we open any of them.

  • An NDA before anything else

    We sign a confidentiality agreement before you send us a single file: yours or ours, whichever you prefer. Everyone who works on your project is bound by it.

  • Your systems first

    Wherever we can, we work inside your own accounts and tools, so your files stay where they already live.

  • Access you control

    You decide what we can open. We ask for the least the task needs, and you can switch it off at any time.

  • No surprise tools

    Before we build, we list every outside service your data would pass through. Nothing is added without your approval.

  • Never used to train AI

    We only use AI services on business terms under which your data is not used to train their models. Your files never go into a personal or free AI account.

  • Deleted when we finish

    When the work ends, we delete our copies of your data within 30 days and confirm it in writing. Ask sooner, and it is done sooner.

  • Never named without permission

    We do not name you as a client, or show your work to anyone else, unless you agree in writing.

  • Told quickly if something goes wrong

    If we learn that your data has been exposed, we tell you within 72 hours: what happened, and what we are doing about it.

These commitments go into the agreement we sign with you. Last updated November 2026.
