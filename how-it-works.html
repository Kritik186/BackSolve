How it works

We start with the result and work backwards.

Most AI projects start with a tool and go looking for a use. We start with what you want, and work back to what needs building.

  1. 1

    Name the result

    Tell us what done looks like.

  2. 2

    Find the loops

    We map how the work gets done today.

  3. 3

    Build the way out

    Short, fixed-scope sprints, inside your tools.

Step 1

Name the result

Tell us what done looks like, before anyone talks about tools.

Example: the Monday report

“Reports out by 9am Monday, and nobody touched them.”

What happens

  1. A 20-minute call about the task your team dreads most.
  2. We agree, in one sentence, what done looks like.
  3. We agree how you will know it worked.

What you leave with

  • One sentence that says what done looks like
  • A straight answer on whether AI can take the task

Step 2

Find the loops

We map how the work gets done today, and rank where AI saves the most time. This is the Loop Audit.

Example: the Monday report

It turns out to be five loops: chase the numbers, retype them, check them, format the pack, send it.

What happens

  1. We sit with the people who do the work and watch how it gets done today.
  2. We write down each step: who does it, how often, and how long it takes.
  3. We rank the loops by hours saved, and by what each fix is worth.
  4. We build one working prototype, so you can judge with something real.

What you leave with

  • A map of how your team's work gets done today
  • Three to five opportunities, ranked by hours saved and payback
  • One working prototype you can try on real work
  • A plan and a fixed quote for building the rest

Step 3

Build the way out

We build it in short, fixed-scope sprints, inside the tools you already use.

Example: the Monday report

Sprint one: the numbers arrive, get checked and land in the pack on their own.

What happens

  1. You choose which loop to fix first.
  2. We agree the scope and a fixed price before any work starts.
  3. We build it inside the tools your team already uses.
  4. Your team tries it on real work, and we adjust it until it holds up.

What you leave with

  • One working automation, in daily use
  • A short guide your team can follow
  • A walkthrough for the people who will use it

What we need from you

  • Someone who does the work

    About [TIME PER WEEK] from a person who does the task today. They show us how it really gets done.

  • Real examples

    Last month's report, a recent memo, the files as they actually arrive. Not a tidied-up version.

  • Access, on your terms

    We sign an NDA first. You decide what we can open, and you can switch it off at any time. How we handle your data

  • Someone who can say yes

    One person who can approve the scope and the price, so nothing stalls.

You do not need technical staff, and you do not need new software.
