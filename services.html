Step 1

The Loop Audit

Find what is worth fixing, and what it is worth. Every client starts here.

1 to 2 weeksFixed fee, agreed on the first call

Book a 20-minute call

What happens

  1. We sit with the people who do the work and watch how it gets done today.
  2. We map each task: who does it, how often, and how long it takes.
  3. We rank where AI saves the most time, and what each fix is worth.
  4. We build one working prototype, so you can judge with something real.

What you get

  • A map of how your team's work gets done today
  • Three to five opportunities, ranked by hours saved and payback
  • One working prototype you can try on real work
  • A plan and a fixed quote for building the rest

What we need from you

About two hours a week from someone on your team, and examples of the real work.

Step 2

Build sprints

Fix it, one fixed-scope sprint at a time. You choose what gets built, and in what order.

Length depends on the taskFixed price per sprint

See example builds

What happens

  1. You pick an opportunity from the audit.
  2. We agree the scope and the price before any work starts.
  3. We build it inside the tools your team already uses.
  4. Your team tries it on real work, and we adjust it until it holds up.

What you get

  • One working automation, in daily use
  • A short guide your team can follow
  • A walkthrough for the people who will use it

What we need from you

Someone who does the task today, to test it with us and tell us where it falls short.

Step 3

Next projects

Come back when you want the next automation built. Each one is its own fixed-price project, and nothing is ongoing unless you ask for it.

Fixed price per projectNo monthly commitment

Book a 20-minute call

What happens

  1. You tell us what you want built next, from your audit list or something new.
  2. We scope it and quote a fixed price before any work starts.
  3. We build it the same way: inside your tools, tested on real work.
  4. If something we built stops working, we fix it. That is included for 60 days after each build.

What you get

  • A new automation, on the same terms as the first
  • A monthly plan if you would rather have us on hand, never as a requirement
  • Fixes and support included for 60 days after each build

What we need from you

Nothing, until you are ready for the next one.
