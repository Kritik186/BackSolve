Use cases

Ten loops, and the way out of each.

Examples of the repeated work we take off small private equity, venture and advisory teams. Pick your kind of firm to see the ones that fit.

Showing 10 of 10

  • Deal screening

    Private equity

    The loop today

    Teasers and information memorandums arrive by email and are read one at a time.

    The way out

    Each one is summarised against your investment criteria and logged in your deal list, with the page that backs every point.

    Stays with your team: Deciding which deals deserve a call.

  • Data room review

    Private equity

    The loop today

    Hundreds of data room files are opened, skimmed and noted by hand.

    The way out

    Ask the data room a question in plain English and get the answer with its file and page. Your standard checklist fills itself in and lists what is missing.

    Stays with your team: Judging what the answers mean for the deal.

  • Investment memos

    Private equity, Venture capital

    The loop today

    First drafts start from a blank page and the last memo.

    The way out

    A first draft in your house format, built from the data room and your model, with every figure linked to where it came from.

    Stays with your team: The argument, the risks and the recommendation.

  • Portfolio reporting

    Private equity, Venture capital

    The loop today

    Monthly numbers are chased from portfolio companies by email, then retyped into the master sheet.

    The way out

    Companies send what they have. The figures are pulled out, checked against last month and dropped into the pack. Late and odd numbers are flagged.

    Stays with your team: The conversation with the company when something looks wrong.

  • Investor reporting

    Private equity, Venture capital

    The loop today

    The quarterly report is assembled by hand from statements, updates and old slides.

    The way out

    A draft in your template, built from numbers the team has already signed off.

    Stays with your team: The message to your investors.

  • Inbound deck screening

    Venture capital

    The loop today

    Inbound decks and emails are screened one at a time, when someone has an hour.

    The way out

    Every deck is summarised against your thesis and logged in the CRM, so partners open a ranked shortlist.

    Stays with your team: Who you take a meeting with.

  • Call notes to CRM

    Venture capital, Advisory firms

    The loop today

    The CRM is updated after every call, when someone remembers.

    The way out

    Notes from each call become a CRM entry, a list of follow-ups and a short summary for the next team meeting.

    Stays with your team: The relationship.

  • Recurring client reports

    Advisory firms

    The loop today

    The Monday report is rebuilt by hand every week.

    The way out

    The report builds itself from the source data and arrives on schedule, with what changed since last time called out.

    Stays with your team: The advice that goes with the numbers.

  • Spreadsheets to slides

    Private equity, Advisory firms

    The loop today

    Numbers are copied from spreadsheets into slides, then copied again when they change.

    The way out

    Charts and tables in the deck update from the model in one step. A check flags any number that no longer matches.

    Stays with your team: The story the slides tell.

  • Data clean-up and matching

    Advisory firms

    The loop today

    Exports from different systems are matched line by line.

    The way out

    Files are matched and reconciled automatically. Only the exceptions come to a person.

    Stays with your team: Deciding what to do about the exceptions.

These are examples of what we build, not client results.
